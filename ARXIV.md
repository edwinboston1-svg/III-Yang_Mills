**What the paper claims, in one sentence for the submitter's own check.** A reflection-positive theory has a mass gap exactly when its reflection-positive correlations cluster exponentially (Theorem 7.1); a lattice gap uniform in physical units along a construction's sequence gives every limit point a unique vacuum, the cluster property and the gap (Theorem 7.4); and that uniform gap follows from two statements about one block at a fixed physical scale, a Dobrushin bound on the blocked measure's coarse variables and the clustering of the field inside the blocks, both uniform in the cutoff (Theorem 8.5). The paper proves these and names the two statements as the open mathematics; it does not claim the gap.
