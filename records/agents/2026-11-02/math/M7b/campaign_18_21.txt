## §20 — THE COARSE HYPOTHESIS FROM THE FIBRE: A REDUCTION, ITS CONSTANT, AND WHY IT DOES NOT CLOSE THE WINDOW FOR GAUGE-COVARIANT COARSE LINKS (2026-11-02 07:3x EST, read from `date`; the trunk seat, at the desk; nothing above edited)

1. **The theorem (proved here).** Work in §8.3's setting, with a block-local coarse map and versions of the conditional laws defined for every coarse configuration.
   - Let ∂y be the fine links outside B_y that share a plaquette with a link of B_y. By the paper's partition they lie in the 80 blocks at distance one.
   - Let p_y(dv_y | ω_∂) be the law of v_y given the links of ∂y. It is a finite pinned object, because the Wilson measure's conditional law of B_y given everything outside depends only on ∂y.
   - Let δ_y = sup_{ω,ω′} ‖p_y(·|ω_∂) − p_y(·|ω′_∂)‖_TV be its ergodicity coefficient.
   - Let T_∂(y, y′) be the supremum, over coarse configurations differing only at y′, of the total variation between the fibre laws ν_j(ω_∂ ∈ · | v).
   Then for y ≠ y′:

   **C^λ_j(y, y′) ≤ δ_y T_∂(y, y′)/(1 − δ_y).**

   *Proof.* Integrate block y's fine field. By the tower and Markov properties, λ(dv_y | v_{−y}) = ∫ p_y(dv_y | ω_∂) μ̃(dω_∂ | v_{−y}), where μ̃ is the law of ω_∂ given the coarse configuration off y, and μ̃(·|v_{−y}) = ∫ ν_j(dω_∂ | w, v_{−y}) λ(dw | v_{−y}). Let v, v′ differ only at y′, and write c for the total variation between λ(·|v_{−y}) and λ(·|v′_{−y}).
   - Dobrushin's contraction gives c ≤ δ_y ‖μ̃ − μ̃′‖.
   - Inserting ∫ ν(·|w, v′_{−y}) λ(dw | v_{−y}) and applying the triangle inequality gives ‖μ̃ − μ̃′‖ ≤ T_∂ + c.
   - Hence c(1 − δ_y) ≤ δ_y T_∂. ∎

   Under Hypothesis II(b), applied to indicator functions of ω_∂ (N(f) ≤ 80, distance at least |y − y′| − 1), T_∂(y, y′) ≤ min(1, 80K′ρ′^{|y−y′|−1}). So **Hypothesis I holds with (κ, α) as soon as sup_y δ_y = δ satisfies δS/(1 − δ) ≤ α < 1**, where S(K′, ρ′, κ) = Σ_{r≥1} n(r) min(1, 80K′ρ′^{r−1}) e^{κr} with n(r) = (2r+1)⁴ − (2r−1)⁴ and ρ′e^κ < 1.
