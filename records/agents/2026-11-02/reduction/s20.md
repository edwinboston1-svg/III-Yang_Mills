

## §20 — THE COARSE HYPOTHESIS FROM THE FIBRE: A REDUCTION, ITS CONSTANT, AND WHY IT DOES NOT CLOSE THE WINDOW FOR GAUGE-COVARIANT COARSE LINKS (2026-11-02 07:3x EST, read from `date`; the trunk seat, at the desk; nothing above edited)

1. **The theorem (proved here).** Work in §8.3's setting, with a block-local coarse map and versions of the conditional laws defined for every coarse configuration.
   - Let ∂y be the fine links outside B_y that share a plaquette with a link of B_y. By the paper's partition they lie in the 80 blocks at distance one.
   - Let p_y(dv_y | ω_∂) be the law of v_y given the links of ∂y. It is a finite pinned object, because the Wilson measure's conditional law of B_y given everything outside depends only on ∂y.
   - Let δ_y = sup_{ω,ω′} ‖p_y(·|ω_∂) − p_y(·|ω′_∂)‖_TV be its ergodicity coefficient.
   - Let T_∂(y, y′) be the supremum, over coarse configurations differing only at y′, of the total variation between the fibre laws ν_j(ω_∂ ∈ · | v).
   Then for y ≠ y′:

   **C^λ_j(y, y′) ≤ δ_y T_∂(y, y′)/(1 − δ_y).**

   *Proof.* Integrate block y's fine field. By the tower and Markov properties, λ(dv_y | v_{−y}) = ∫ p_y(dv_y | ω_∂) μ̃(dω_∂ | v_{−y}), where μ̃ is the law of ω_∂ given the coarse configuration off y, and μ̃(·|v_{−y}) = ∫ ν_j(dω_∂ | w, v_{−y}) λ(dw | v_{−y}). Let v, v′ differ only at y′, and write c for the total variation between λ(·|v_{−y}) and λ(·|v′_{−y}).
   - Dobrushin's contraction gives c ≤ δ_y ‖μ̃ − μ̃′‖.
   - Inserting ∫ ν(·|w, v′_{−y}) λ(dw | v_{−y}) and applying the triangle inequality gives ‖μ̃ − μ̃′‖ ≤ T_∂ + c.
   - Hence c(1 − δ_y) ≤ δ_y T_∂. ∎

   Under Hypothesis II(b), applied to indicator functions of ω_∂ (N(f) ≤ 80, distance at least |y − y′| − 1), T_∂(y, y′) ≤ min(1, 80K′ρ′^{|y−y′|−1}). So **Hypothesis I holds with (κ, α) as soon as sup_y δ_y = δ satisfies δS/(1 − δ) ≤ α < 1**, where S(K′, ρ′, κ) = Σ_{r≥1} n(r) min(1, 80K′ρ′^{r−1}) e^{κr} with n(r) = (2r+1)⁴ − (2r−1)⁴ and ρ′e^κ < 1. Theorem 8.5 then holds from Hypothesis II alone plus the one pinned number δ.

3. **Two-sided reading.**
   - With the holonomy map, δ → 0 along the sequence because the coarse variable equidistributes. Hypothesis I then holds trivially and all content sits in II(a), exactly the diagnosis of revision 32.
   - For U(1) in the Coulomb phase, the fibre has no long modes: the coarse variables carry them, and the zero-block-average modes have the block-scale Laplacian gap. So II plausibly holds for both groups. The group dependence then sits in δ, since the coarse variable's law becomes insensitive to its boundary only where the coupling has run to strong coupling.

4. **Why it does not close the window, the pre-mortem done before landing.** δ is a supremum over PINNED fine data, and for gauge-covariant coarse links it is of order one at every scale. The paper's coarse link, the holonomy along a block edge in the block's fixed gauge, and every block-local link variable run through links adjacent to ∂y. Pinned boundary data can therefore align those links: flat data pull them toward the identity, with an effective field of order β times the number of straddling plaquettes. The data can also rotate them, since a gauge transformation of the data at the boundary sites rotates the law of v_y. So p_y(·|ω_∂) is far from Haar and depends strongly on ω_∂, even where λ(dv_y | v_{−y}) is near Haar. The blocked measure's small influence at a strongly coupled coarse scale comes from AVERAGING over the fibre's strongly fluctuating boundary layer, which the pinned supremum discards. This is the proof agent M2b's distinction carried to the coarse kernel: averaging is legitimate for what is integrated. The pinned sensitivity of the gauge-INVARIANT coarse loop is small at two steps from 2.2: E[χ_½(A)] lies between 0.005 and 0.146 at the read data, so δ_loop ≥ 0.035. But loops are not a product space, and Dobrushin's comparison needs one.

5. **What this places.** Every route through a pinned supremum of a gauge-covariant coarse variable is closed by item 4: the tower lemma, this reduction, and fixed-datum influence readings. The window's difficulty is the AVERAGING over the fibre of gauge-covariant coarse links at O(1) coupling. The next objects are:
   - a fibre-averaged δ, the boundary-layer law integrated against the fibre given the coarse data, bounded through Hypothesis II itself (a self-consistent form δ ≤ f(K′, ρ′));
   - or a gauge-invariant reformulation of Theorem 8.5's comparison step on coarse loops, where pinned sensitivities are small.
   The second is the L² currency's natural home: the reduction in L² (Theorem 8.5′) works with conditional maximal correlations of coarse observables. The proof agents M5 ((W-V)) and M2b ((W-S)) are on the two channels of that form.
