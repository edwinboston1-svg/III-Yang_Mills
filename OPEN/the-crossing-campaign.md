## §0 — THE GOAL IN THE EXACT WORDS, AND THE SHAPE OF THE PROOF

**The prize.** For every compact simple gauge group G, a non-trivial quantum Yang–Mills theory exists on ℝ⁴ (the Wightman/Osterwalder–Schrader axioms with the theory's local gauge-invariant observables) and has a mass gap Δ > 0.

**The shape in hand (THE GROUND §7–8, THE FAR SHORE §2).** The continuum gap of every limit point of a construction of the corpus's kind follows from two finite statements at one fixed physical scale a_c (Theorem 8.5): Hypothesis I, the blocked measure's coarse variables under a Dobrushin-type condition with an exponential weight, uniformly in the cutoff; Hypothesis II, the field inside a block clusters at the block scale (exact under the perfect decimation). The chain MassGapChain.lean closes the gap with one rate for every cutoff from the UV interface (every effective weight at a_c a tilt of one base weight) and the crossover budget. The flux-surface gas (Theorem 8.9) is a convergent expansion for β ≤ β* = 0.4764 with a certified margin.

**The one open link, stated at its sharpest (THE FAR SHORE §4.1, §10).** The window theorem: the blocked theory crosses the couplings of order one, [β_D, β_P], with its correlation length finite at the exit. In the lemma's form: at an exit scale where the blocked Wilson coupling is β_c ≤ β*, the blocked action's non-Wilson content c(β_c) lies under the gas's exit budget ε(β_c), uniformly in the cutoff. ε is certified (§10 RESULTS 1: 2.32 at 0.05, 0.68 at 0.25, 0.00 at β*). c is unread. The campaign is the proof that c ≤ ε at some exit, for SU(2) first, for every compact simple group last.

**The two controls that bind every stage.** (F1) four-dimensional U(1) at weak coupling has no gap and no exit scale: every stage must REFUSE it — a stage whose instrument or theorem treats U(1) as SU(2) is withdrawn at that stage. (F2) the free massless Gaussian field: algebraic correlations with no frustration; any criterion monotone in averaged per-site influences certifies it falsely.
