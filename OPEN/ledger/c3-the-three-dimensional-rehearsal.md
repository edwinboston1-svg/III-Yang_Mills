# C3 — Theorem 8.7 in three dimensions: the cover, the counts, the row, two roads each (06:24 by the clock)

**The theorem (to enter the paper as Theorem 8.8 in revision 24).** SU(2) Wilson lattice gauge theory on ℤ³ at β ≤ 0.3739 (0.3841 with the exact one-face constants) has a unique infinite-volume Gibbs measure, exponential clustering of local gauge-invariant observables and a transfer-matrix mass gap. The proof is Theorem 8.7's with the three-dimensional objects: a perfect cover of ℤ³ of period 2 (every plaquette containing exactly one cover link; six cover links per eight sites, a quarter of all links, as the count D/4 per site requires), P = 4 plaquettes per link, 8 direct and 36 mediated entries in the kept links' Dobrushin row, the fibre arguments 3β and 4β, and the per-plaquette oscillation term β²D/2 + β³T/3 summed over the link's four plaquettes.

**The cover, two roads.** iii-38's Algorithm X (`g37_cover_dim.pl`, 5fc71fbe…) found it: period 2 × 2 × 2, six links over eight sites — (0,0,0)+e₁, (0,0,1)+e₂, (0,1,1)+e₂, (1,0,0)+e₁, (1,1,0)+e₃, (1,1,1)+e₃, coordinates mod 2 — the sites (0,1,0) and (1,0,1) carrying none; its tiled recount (`g37_tile_check.pl`, 35115db0…) lists every plaquette's four links afresh on (ℤ/2)³, (ℤ/4)³, (ℤ/6)³, (ℤ/8)³ (24, 192, 648, 1536 plaquettes) and finds none with a count other than one. iii-43's enumeration (`records/second-road/coverrule3d.py`, sharing no line) on the same four tori: zero bad plaquettes; the control with one link added bleeds 4 at side 2. The 4D period-2 cover (the paper's sixteen) and the 2D case are the instrument's known answers.

**The counts.** Enumerated from the covers past the wrap (period 4 and beyond): DIRECT 2P = 4(D − 1), MEDIATED 3P(P − 1) = 6(D − 1)(2D − 3) — 12/90 in four dimensions (the paper's), 8/36 in three, 4/6 in two; at period 2 the torus identifies neighbours (88 in 4D, 34–36 in 3D), so the counts are read past it.

**The row, two roads.** The proof's constants carry no dimension except through P: ω = 3β²D₅ + 2β³T₆ is the sum over six plaquettes of β²D/2 + β³T/3 (the proof moves each h_p by "a function of oscillation at most β²D₅ + (2/3)β³T₆", its quadratic part by min(β²D₅, 2β³T₆), its remainder by (2/3)β³T₆ — per plaquette, none a count), so in dimension D ω = P(β²D/2 + β³T/3) with D = m((P−1)β)²/3 and T = m(Pβ)/2 (hand) or |D((P−1)β)| and |φ‴(Pβ)| (exact). iii-38's `g38_row_dim.awk` (5f584175…): the 4D known answer ROW(0.2410) = 0.996296731 met; the 3D crossing 0.3739 (ROW 0.999547 at 0.3739 = 0.3668 direct + 0.6327 mediated); 2D 0.8252. iii-43's `records/second-road/rowdim.py` (Decimal, 60 digits, sharing no line): KAT 0.996296731; D = 3: ROW(0.37) = 0.976276, ROW(0.374) = 1.000148, ROW = 1 at β = 0.373975 (hand) and 0.384144 (exact); D = 2: 0.825209 / 0.896545; D = 4: 0.241415 / 0.245263. The two roads agree to every printed digit.

**What is new and what is not.** The three-dimensional lattice gap at strong coupling is classical in existence (the cluster expansion); the coupling 0.3739 and its certification are this work's. Three dimensions is where the ultraviolet half is in print without a sequence hypothesis (Bałaban, CMP 102 (1985)), so the same crossover question — the interval of the effective coupling between the certified disk and the small-field foothold — is posed there with both ends published, which is why the plan's C3 and D3 call it the rehearsal. Owed before the paper: nothing for the theorem; for its record, the outside test the 4D row had (g23's search from below), staked first.

**The cover in closed form (06:37 by the clock).** iii-38's six links over the 2 × 2 × 2 period are the links (x, e₁) with x₂ and x₃ even, (x, e₂) with x₁ even and x₃ odd, and (x, e₃) with x₁ and x₂ odd: three families of parallel lines. Proof that every plaquette meets exactly one: in the (1, 2)-plane at base point x the two e₁-links sit at second coordinates x₂ and x₂ + 1 with the same x₃, so exactly one is a cover link when x₃ is even and none when it is odd, while the two e₂-links sit at first coordinates x₁ and x₁ + 1 with the same x₃, so exactly one is a cover link when x₃ is odd and none when it is even; in the (1, 3)-plane the e₁-links give one exactly when x₂ is even and the e₃-links one exactly when x₂ is odd; in the (2, 3)-plane the e₂-links give one exactly when x₁ is even and the e₃-links one exactly when x₁ is odd. A cover link count of D/4 per site follows from D(D − 1)/2 plaquettes per site and 2(D − 1) plaquettes per link.

**Against the explicit threshold of record.** Shen, Zhu and Zhu (*Comm. Math. Phys.* 2023, doi:10.1007/s00220-022-04609-1; arXiv:2204.12737, their (1.2)–(1.3)) prove uniqueness, functional inequalities and a mass gap for |β| < 1/(16(d − 1)) with the action Nβ Re Σ Tr Q_p, so their β is one quarter of Wilson's for SU(2): β < 1/12 on ℤ⁴ and β < 1/8 on ℤ³ in Wilson's normalisation. Theorem 8.7 reaches 0.2452 on ℤ⁴ and Theorem 8.8 0.3739 on ℤ³ (0.3841 with the exact constants), both about three times as far.
