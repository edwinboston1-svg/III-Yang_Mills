Hypothesis I follows from Hypothesis II(b) and the coarse kernel's ergodicity coefficient δ once δS/(1 − δ) < 1; the threshold δ* is about 0.003–0.005 for a fibre decaying by e⁻⁸ per coarse unit. For gauge-covariant coarse links δ is of order one at every scale, because pinned boundary data align and gauge-rotate the block-edge links. The coarse measure's small influence comes from averaging over the fibre, which a pinned supremum discards.
